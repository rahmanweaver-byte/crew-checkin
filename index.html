Daily times

Makes the MUSTER and DCTTS for the day shown above from the saved workbooks: IN and OUT on the MUSTER and START TIME on the DCTTS, filled in from the scans and approved corrections. Hours, meal breaks, and signatures stay blank for the paper copy. When you print the DCTTS, choose Print Entire Workbook in Excel’s print screen so all of that day’s sheets come out.

Week of

Hours from scans each day, with timesheet hours beside them once you import them.

Paste rows from the timesheet export: date, employee #, hours. Several rows for the same person and day are added together. Importing a day again replaces that person’s hours for the day.

Corrections

The only way to change a time. Each fix needs a reason and counts once someone approves it; the original scan stays on record.

Roster

One person per line: employee #, name, role, company, crew. Copying rows straight out of Excel works. Anyone already on the roster gets updated.

Tap a person to edit them. Set someone inactive instead of removing them, so their past scans keep their name.

Station

Settings for the app and for this tablet.

Hours run from the first check-in to the last check-out. Lunch comes off for anyone on site when it starts: someone who leaves before then gets no lunch, and someone who leaves during it gets lunch only until they leave. With the end-of-shift rule on, a check-out at or after the second time counts as the end of shift, an earlier check-out keeps its real time, and an approved correction overrides both.
